## Mutated Parts
- Certain effects may reference mutated [[Human Body|Body Parts]]. These body parts are not [[Cybernetics]] nor [[Human Body#Natural Body Parts|Natural Body Parts]]. 
- Mutated parts give [[Cybernetics#Cyber Modifier|Cyber Modifier]], dont have a [[Cybernetics|Cybercost]], but do have varied [[Cybernetics#Affected Skills/Ability Scores|Cyber Modifier Multipliers]], typically with some skills positive and others negative. 
- Mutated parts have effects that may be positive or negative, all mutated parts have atleast one of each.
