## Neural Heat Overview
- Anytime you cast a psycast, you generate an amount of neural heat. Every turn, some of your generated neural heat dissipates. Neural heat is the primary limiter of how many psycasts may be cast in a short amount of time. 
## Neural Heat Limit
- The base neural heat limit is 30 and is a soft limit that may be exceeded. Psycasters who exceed this limit take certain risks as seen in [[Exceeding the Neural Heat Limit]].
## Neural Heat Dissipation
- Neural heat dissipates over rapidly over time, at the beginning of a creature's turn, they lose 5 generated neural heat. 
	- The amount lost may be increased or decreased by effects.
