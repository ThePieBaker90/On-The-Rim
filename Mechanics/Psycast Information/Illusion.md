- An illusion may be created by a psycast (or some other effect). They are the falsification of senses and are highly malleable. 
- Illusions are only perceived if an effect specifies a creature will perceive it and only affect the senses chosen for that illusion. Illusions take a form of its creators choosing in a location (or locations) of its creator's choice.
- When you create an illusion, you choose each of the following:
	- Which senses the illusion will affect out of sight, hearing, smell, taste, or touch.
		- An illusion that affects sight produces the sight of an image, object, or other visual stimuli located in a position.
		- An illusion that affects hearing produces a sound, be it a song, spoken words, a chime or some other auditory stimuli from a point.
		- An illusion that affects smell produces a scent such as a strong odor, a faint whiff, or some other olfactory stimuli from an area.
		- An illusion that affects taste produces a flavor such as a sour fruit, a bland lack of taste, or some other gustatory stimuli on a trigger.
		- An illusion that affects touch produces a tactile stimuli over an area. Creatures must intentionally push through touch illusions rather than phasing immediately through them.
		- When an illusion is created, the creating effect may specify constraints on the number of different senses that can be affected.
	- What the illusion will be perceived as concerning the chosen senses.
		- If an illusion affects a sense, it may affect that sense in multiple ways.
			- For example, an illusion that affects hearing can be perceived as soft chimes from one point and a snare drum from another point.
	- Where the illusion will be perceived.
		- Where an illusion affects a sense does not need to be the same for every instance of that sense.
			- For example, a flavor may be perceived upon tasting a chosen piece of food and a different flavor may be perceived upon tasting a different piece of food.
- The illusion takes on the chosen properties for the duration of its lifetime.
- An illusion may be investigated by a creature skeptic of its legitimacy. As an action, they may make an Intelligence (investigation) skill check on the illusion to discern its legitimacy.
	- On a failure, they continue believing the illusion is real.
	- On a success, they discern that it is an illusion.
