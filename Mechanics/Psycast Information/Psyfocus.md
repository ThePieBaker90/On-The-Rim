## Psyfocus Overview
- Anytime you use a psycast, it costs a certain amount of psyfocus to cast it. Psyfocus is the primary limiter of how many powerful psycasts can be cast throughout a day.
## Psyfocus Storage
- The base amount of psyfocus a psycaster can store is 100. This limit increases as they level up.
## Restoring Psyfocus
- Psyfocus is restored when you take a rest, to the maximum amount you can store. The amount restored can be changed by effects.
