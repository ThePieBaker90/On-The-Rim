## Gravity Overview
- Gravity is a force which draws objects towards each other. Objects with a higher mass have a higher gravitational pull. 
- Gravity typically pulls creatures to the ground of a planet or to a surface with effects that manipulate gravity. 
- Gravitational effects take place over an area, pulling or pushing a creature towards a point or direction. If multiple areas overlap, both forces apply, either adding together, not affecting the other, or countering the other.
