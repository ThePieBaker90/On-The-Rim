## Vacuum Overview
-  An area may be designated as a vacuum, such as the vacuum of space or a vacuum chamber. An area that is a vacuum has dangerous effects on exposed creatures within.
## Vacuum Effects
- When a creature is in a vacuum, they suffer the following effects at the beginning of each of their turns:
	- They gain 1 level of [[Vacuum Exposure]].
	- If they are [[Breathing#Holding your Breath|Holding Their Breath]], both of their [[Lung|Lungs]] gain 5 [[Injury]].
