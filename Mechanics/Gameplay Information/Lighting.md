- Light allows creatures to see, it can be created by many different sources such as a star, a candle, or a [[Psycasts|Psycast]] such as [[Create Light]]. It can also be removed, such as with the [[Void Shroud]] [[Psycasts|Psycast]].
- Every area has an associated [[#Light Levels|Light Level]] which determines effects of creatures standing in that area.
## Light Levels
- Light levels are a sliding scale which may be modified by effects, the scale ranges from 5 to 1. An effect may add or subtract from an area's light level. When it does this, the area's light changes to the new level.
	- 5 - [[#Bright Light]]
	- 4 - [[#Normal Light]]
	- 3 - [[#Dim Light]]
	- 2 - [[#Darkness]]
	- 1 - [[#Void]]
- If an area has two light sources affecting it, the light level is determined by the light source with the highest light level after modifiers.
## Bright Light
- Bright light is created by very powerful sources such as a planet's nearby star, flares, or other things which create light but should not be directly looked at.
- Typically, creatures can see into and within bright light clearly. 
	- [[Stealth and Hiding]] is nearly impossible without cover while in bright light.
## Normal Light
- Normal light is created by most sources of light such as [[Create Light]], flashlights, fire, or other things which create a moderate amount of light.
- Typically, creatures can see into and within normal light clearly. 
	- [[Stealth and Hiding]] is difficult without cover while in normal light.
## Dim Light
- Dim light is created by very few sources such as screens or moonlight. Dim light is most often created on the barrier between normal light and darkness.
- Typically, creatures can see somewhat into dim light.
	- [[Stealth and Hiding]] is average difficulty while in dim light.
	- Disadvantage on [[Sight-Based Perception]] checks into dim light.
- Creatures with [[Darkvision]] can see into this as if it was [[#Normal Light]].
## Darkness
- Darkness is created when light is absent from an area. 
- Typically, creatures cannot see at all into darkness.
	- They automatically fail all [[Sight-Based Perception]] checks into dim light.
	- Attack rolls on them have advantage.
	- They have disadvantage on attack rolls.
- Creatures with [[Darkvision (30ft)|Darkvision Genes]] can see into this as if it was monochromatic [[#Dim Light]].
## Void
- Void is created by very few effects such as [[Void Shroud]] or [[Ball 'o' Black]].
- With the exception of  a few creatures, all creatures cannot see at all into void.
	- They automatically fail all [[Sight-Based Perception]] checks into dim light.
	- Attack rolls on them have advantage.
	- They have disadvantage on attack rolls.
