## Weapon Properties Overview
- Weapons may have several properties listed, these properties are effects that the weapon has that differentiate it from other weapons. Some effects may be passive effects while others need to be triggered (such as landing a successful hit or dealing damage with the weapon).
	- Each property has its own page detailing its effects.
- Some weapons may have a "special" property, this means that thew weapon has unique effects which are detailed in the weapon's special field.
