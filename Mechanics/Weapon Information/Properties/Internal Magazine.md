## General Description
Some weapons have a built-in magazine instead of a detachable external one. These are reloaded not by swapping the magazine but by loading ammunition into the magazine, individually or using clips or another sort of speedloader.
Any ranged weapon without an external magazine should be given this property even if it doesn't actually have a true internal magazine- e.g. revolvers or break-action weapons.

## Mechanics
- In the inventory, ammunition is stored in clips of a specific size. See inventory (tbd)
- Ammunition can typically be reloaded with individual rounds or with clips (a generic term from hear for speadloaders)
- If the available empty ammunition capacity of the magazine is less than the capacity of the clip the clip cannot be used to reload the magazine
  - Example: if a 10-round internal magazine has 7 rounds loaded a 5-round clip cannot be used to reload
- Rounds are fired in a last-in first-out order relative to how they are loaded
