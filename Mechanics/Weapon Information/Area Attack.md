## General Description
Some weapons, rather than attacking one target, will attack a cone or area. This is different from a point. Attacks on a point use normal attack rules or may have different rules specified by the weapon.

## Mechanics
- The area or cone for the attack will be specified by the weapon.
- All creatures within this area or cone and not behind sufficiently hard cover make a dexterity save to avoid being hit.
  - Relevant obscuration modifier is added to this roll.
  - If target AV is greater than weapon AP, the roll is made with advantage.
  - If target AV is greater than or equal to double the weapon's AP, the save automatically succeeds.
  - The target may drop prone to add an advantage to the roll.
- On a sucess, the target is not hit.
- In order of lowest roll to highest roll, the targets that failed the save roll a die or dice to determine how many times they were hit by the area attack.
  - Typically 1d4-1.
- The number of hits is capped by the number of ammunition expended by the attack. If that number is reached and there are still targets that failed the roll, they  are not hit. Similarly, if the hit roll for a target would cause more hits than ammunition expended, use instead the remainder of ammunition expended.
- Finally, roll the appropriate damage die or dice for each hit.
