## General Description
A character can brace, placing themselves in an optimal firing stance to manage recoil. This can look like simply a steady firing stance, propping the weapon on some surface, utilizing a bipod, or something else. 

## Mechanics
- The user may expend an action and 10ft of movement to Brace. They gain the Braced condition.
- Bracing while prone is a free action and requires no movement.
- Bracing while utilizing a bipod or other mount is a free action but retains the movement requirement.
- If using a one-handed weapon with two hands, Bracing is a free action but retains the movement requirement.
- The user retains the Braced condition until they move or take an action that would logically take them out of their braced stance.

## The Braced Condition
- Recoil is reduced by 2
  - In the case of two-handed being used with one hand, this effect is applied after the recoil is doubled (to prevent an effective -4 to recoil)
- Negates one level of [WIP, that innacuracy thing](WIP)
- Weapons, abilitied, etc. may specify additional effects of being Braced. For example, some particularly heavy weapons may require bracing to use.
