## General Description
Ordinary firearm usage sees the operator stop, aim down sights, and then attack the target. It is also possible to skip either or both of these initial steps. Generally, this is called hipfiring. Based on the power of the weapon, it is more or less difficult to accurately hit a target while hipfiring.

## Mechanics
- Certain effects can apply a level of Hipfire Penalty. These levels stack to a maximum of 3.
- For each level of Hipfire Penalty, subtract one of the weapon's primary damage die from the hit roll.
  - For weapons with multiple possible damage dice, the one to use for the Hipfire Penalty should be specified.

## An Example
- An attack is made with one level of Hipfire Penalty with an rifle that deals 2d6 damage per bullet
- The hit roll becomes 1d20-1d6, as the d6 is the rifle's primary damage die
  - The d20 is a 14 and the d6 a 3, so the result is 14-3=11
