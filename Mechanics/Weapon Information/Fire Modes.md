## Description
A weapon, particularly a firearm, may have multiple fire modes. These change how the weapon's attack works. Unless otherwise specified, switching between fire modes is a free action.

## Common Examples
This is not an exhaustive list of fire modes. If a weapon has a fire mode not on this list it should be described within the weapon's page. 
- Semiautomatic: One ammunition is fired per attack action.
- Burst: A fixed amount of ammunition, described on the weapon's page (e.g. Burst:3), is fired per attack action. Each ammunition fired is treated as a separate attack with independent hit rolls, damage calculations, or other effects.
- Automatic: A die or dice roll amount of ammunition is fired per attack action, described on the weapon's page (e.g. 1d4), and this number may be modified by up to the shooter's firearm skill modifier (e.g. 1D4 +-2). Each ammunition fired is treated as a separate attack with independent hit rolls, damage calculations, or other effects.

- Safe: The weapon cannot be fired in safe mode.
