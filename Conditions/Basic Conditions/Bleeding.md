- When a creature has the bleeding condition, they suffer the following effects:
	- They lose 100ml of [[Body/Humanoid Body Parts/Cardiovascular System/Blood|Blood]] per turn.
	- They lose 3 health per turn or they lose 1% of their maximum health worth of health per turn, whichever is greater.
	- If they are healed, the duration of bleeding reduces by 1 turn per 10 health healed. 
		- All healing reduces the duration of bleeding by a minimum of 1 turn on them.
	- If they are [[Dying]], they automatically add 1 failed throw per turn.
- If this condition is applied to a creature who already has this condition, the durations of both are added to determine the new duration.
- Constructs are immune to bleeding unless otherwise stated.
