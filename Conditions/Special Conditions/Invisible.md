- When a creature has the invisible condition, you have the following effects:
	- You are [[Stealth and Hiding#Hidden|Hidden]] from all creatures and cannot be [[Stealth and Hiding#Observed|Observed]].
		- You may become [[Stealth and Hiding#Undetected|Undetected]] or [[Stealth and Hiding#Unnoticed|Unnoticed]].
	- You cannot be seen and cannot be affected by effects which require you to be seen to target you.
