- Hemorrhage always has an indefinite duration.
- When a creature has the hemorrhage status, they suffer the following effects:
	- They are [[Bleeding]].
	- They lose 100ml of [[Body/Humanoid Body Parts/Cardiovascular System/Blood|Blood]] per turn.
	- If they are [[Dying]], they automatically add 1 failed throw per turn.
- Constructs are immune to hemorrhage unless otherwise stated.
