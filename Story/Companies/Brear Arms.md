# Brear Arms
## General Description
Brear Arms is a major military contractor. Originally started to sell firearms, the company since expanded with divisions selling a variety of military vehicles and equipment. The main arms division holds a dominant position in the market for mediumn and heavy machine guns and also has successful lines of handguns and RPGs. Brear Arms is also the primary manufacturer of 5.45 Empire and 12.7 Empire.


### OOU/OTT Notes
Weapons made by this company should generally have their appearance based on iconic American and Western weapons from the 50s or earlier. Naming convention is a two-letter abbreviation, dash, number, letter: Weapon Type, Model number, Version letter. Additional colloquial name afterward optional.
