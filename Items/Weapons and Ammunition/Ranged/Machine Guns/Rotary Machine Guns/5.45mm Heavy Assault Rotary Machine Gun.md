## General Description
The 5.45mm Heavy Assault Rotary Machine Gun, colloquially known as "Dakka" or "Sasha" is a low-calibre portable rotary machine gun chambered in 5.45 Empire. It offers a blistering rate of fire that allows the user to lay down a veritable lead curtain of suppressive fire. This rate of fire does come at a high cost, however. Quite literally, the amount of ammunition consumed by this weapon is enormous; it takes 400 credits to fire this weapon for 12 seconds. While very compact for a rotary machine gun, the 545HA is still an extremely bulky, heavy weapon and requires significant size and strength to wield effectively.

## Visual Description
The 5.45HA is a bulky weapon constructed of matte black plas-steel. With its six barrels and bulky carry frame, the weapon seems almost stumpy. However, each barrel is actually longer than that of a typical assault rifle. Typically, ammunition is stored in an underslung enclosed helical ammunition belt. When spooling up the whir of the weapon's internal motor has been described as similar to a large hornet, and the sound of the weapon firing is sometimes compared to "gravel in a blender". 

## Lore
"There's nothing mini about this gun!" - ZIA advertisment tagline.
The 5.45mm Heavy Assault was developed by Zulu Industrial Armaments in 5517 with the goal of providing portable heavy weapons fire. Due to the size and expense of the weapon, it has seen limited success compared to more practical conventional light machine guns. However, the 5.45HA has become infamous among collectors and enthusiasts as the ultamite heavy weapon for a heavy weapons guy.
