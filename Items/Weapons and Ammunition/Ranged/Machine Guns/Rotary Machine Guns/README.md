Rotary Machine Guns, RMGs, or miniguns, are a special type of machine gun utilizing multiple barrels that fire in turn while rotating. This configuration allows for an unmatched rate of fire and excellent reliability and heat management charateristics, but also results in a large and heavy weapon poorly suited for accurate fire. RMGs also uniquely must be spun up prior to firing. 
- Typical die size: D6, D8, D12
- Typical RoF: area fire
- Typical capacity: 200
- Recoil: area fire
- Range: area fire
