## General Description
35x220mm Empire or 35mm Empire is a heavy cartridge primarily used for autocannons, though it also sees occasional use with wheeled anti-tank cannons. Considerably more powerful but also considerably more bulky than 20mm Empire, 35mm Empire is a threat to be reckoned with for any target. It lacks the penetration to defeat a tank's frontal armour, but otherwise its penetration is sufficient for any target. It is popularly used for IFVs, anti-aircraft vehicles, mobile gun platforms, and aircraft.
