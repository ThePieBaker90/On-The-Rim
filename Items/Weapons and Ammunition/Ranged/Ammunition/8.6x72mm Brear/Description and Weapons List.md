## General Description
8.6x72mm Brear is a popular high-power rimmed rifle cartridge developed by Brear Arms. It is highly popular in both military and civilian use for high-accuracy long-range weapons. However, the cartridge's rimmed design complicates feeding for automatic weapons, and it occupies an awkward position with higher recoil than 7.92 Empire and comparable cartides but lower power than 12.7 Empire and comparable cartridges. As such it's rare to see high-rate-of-fire weapons chambered in 8.6x72mm Brear.
