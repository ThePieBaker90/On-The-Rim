## General Description
20x105mm Empire or simply 20mm Empire is a light and versatile cannon cartridge. Some personal weapons do use this cartridge, but it is primarily used with autocannons on ground or air vehicles or anti-aircraft mounts. For a cannon it's a relatively lightweight and low-power cartridge, but even a small cannon is still a cannon. Against infantry and lightly-armoured targets, 20mm autocannons are devastatibg, but heavy vehicles or fortificatinons are likely to shrug off the *comparatively* low penetration.
