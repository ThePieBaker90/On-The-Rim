## General Description
12.7x99mm Empire or simply 12.7 Empire is a high-power rifle cartridge. It has been in production for an extraordinary amount of time, some say it's even pre-Calamity. But if it ain't broke, don't fix it. 12.7 Empire is highly regarded for its power and precision while retaining reasonable portability.
