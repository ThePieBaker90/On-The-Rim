## General Description
6.75x50mm Praetoria is an advanced intermediary cartridge developed sometime in the late 5510s. It offers an unrivalled combination of power, versatility, and compact size. Its exact design is classified but the use of composite materials and plasteel within the casing is assumed. Its use is strictly exclusive to the elite Imperial Gaurds units, with Praetoria and the weapons that use it being symbols of Imperial dominance. Punishment for possesion, distribution, or aquisition, or attempts thereof, being death- or worse.
