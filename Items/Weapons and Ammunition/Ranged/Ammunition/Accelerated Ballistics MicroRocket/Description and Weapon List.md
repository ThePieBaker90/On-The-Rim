## General Description
The Accelerated Ballistics MicroRocket is a highly unusual cartridge. Instead of using an explosion to propel an inert projectile, each MicroRocket is a small rocket-propelled projectile that continues burn after leaving the barrel of the weapon, similar in operation to Accelerated Ballistic's popular rocket-assisted recoilless rifles but at a much smaller scale. This system eliminates recoil and is quite lightweight and low-complexity, but is generally innaccurate and considerably more expensive than conventional ammunition.
