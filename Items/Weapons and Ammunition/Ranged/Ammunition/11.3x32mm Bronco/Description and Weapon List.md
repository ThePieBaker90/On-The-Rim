## General Description
A powerful rimmed centerfire cartridge, popular for revolvers and also used in some high-powered pistols. As the cartridge-of-choice for use in revolvers it's largely associated with pioneers and first colonists on the edges of human space, where the reliability and stopping power of a revolver are desirable over the fire rate and portability of a conventional pistol.
