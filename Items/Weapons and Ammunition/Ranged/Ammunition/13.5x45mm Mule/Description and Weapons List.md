## General Description
The 13.5x45mm Magnum cartridge is a massive centerfire rimmed cartridge, essentially a scaled up 11.3x32mm Bronco, intended for use in exceptionally large and powerful pistols and revolvers. These weapons colloquially named "hand cannons" represent the upper end of the size and power of single-handed weapons, rivalling that of many rifles. The trade-off for this immense power is the sheer bulk of the cartridge and its immense recoil- as the marketing says, "it kicks like no tomorrow."
