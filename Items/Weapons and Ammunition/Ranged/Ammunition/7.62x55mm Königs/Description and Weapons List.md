## General Description
7.62x55mm Königs, or Königs Long, is Königs Arms' proprietary full-power rifle cartridge. Whereas Königs Short differs significantly from its primary competitor 5.45 Empire, Königs Long is more or less identical in performance to 7.92 Empire. In fact, in the Outer Regions it is somewhat common to see weapons originally chambered for 7.92 Empire rechambered for Königs Long. There are rifles designed for Königs Long, but the cartridge is much more commonly found in use with various machine guns.
