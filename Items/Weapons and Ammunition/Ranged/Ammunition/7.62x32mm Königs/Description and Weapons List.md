## General Description
7.62x32mm Königs, also known as Königs Short, is Königs Arms' proprietary intermediate cartridge. Firing a relatively havey bullet at relatively low velocity, weapons using Königs Short typically have higher penetration and slightly increased stopping power compared to weapons chambered in 5.45 Empire, while having a lower range and capacity. As with all Königs cartridges the use of Königs Short is more common in the Outer Regions.
