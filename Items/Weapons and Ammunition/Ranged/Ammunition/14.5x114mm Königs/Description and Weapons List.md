## General Description
14.5x114mm Königs or Königs Heavy is Königs Arm's proprietary antimateriel cartridge, though it's typically advertized as "antitank". The cartridge is moderately more powerful than standard 12.7 Empire ammunition though only slightly more effective than 12.7HP. Where Königs Heavy really shines is at extreme ranges, as the massive bullets retain momentum extremely well.
