## General Description
2-bore is an enourmous shotgun cartridge that blurs the line with a smoothbore cannon with its enourmous 51mm calibre. Thanks to its large size ammunition for this cartridge can be made with a variety of specialized payloads. Weapons using this cartridge are rare as the sheer weight and recoil force make it entirely impractical for personal use.
