## General Description
The Brear Arms M5119 is a pistol chambered in 11.3 Bronco. Powerful and robust with a simple and efficient design, the M119 was once the Empire standard secondary arm and is still widely popular today. However, with its relatively low capacity it has since lost popularity compared to the Steader P5330.

## Lore
Development of the M119 began in 5106. The Manlich Revolt of the late 51st century had revealed inadequacies in the standard Imperial arms as the stopping power of the 6x45mm Kreig rifle cartridge and 4.5x31mm Standard pistol cartridge were insufficient to put down the heavily cybernetically augmented opposition. The former failure would be solved by the development of a new cartridge- 7.92x57mm Empire. The latter failure was still open for a solution. Brear Arms, having recently developed the 11.3x32mm Bronco cartridge for use in revolvers, decided to attempt to develop a semi-automatic pistol to fire said cartridge. Despite the significant challenges of creating a magazine feed for a rimmed cartridge, the resulting pistol performed excellently and after a short competition with other bids by Kreig and Armaments and Technology Inc. the Brear M119 was selected to serve as the standard service pistol for the Imperial Army.
