Assault rifles are automatic rifles firing intermediate-power ammunition. They blend power, rate of fire, portability, capacity, and handling into a highly versatile package that has made this weapon class arguably the most popular primary weapon type. However, while they do everything well there's not really any area where they excel. Some say a jack of all trades master of none is better than a master of one, but that only holds true until a master is needed. 

Typical die size: D6
Typical RoF: 3
Typical capacity: 20
Recoil: moderate
Range: medium
