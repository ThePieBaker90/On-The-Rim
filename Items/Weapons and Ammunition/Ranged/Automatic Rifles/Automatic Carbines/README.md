Automatic carbines are small compact automatic rifles. Firing low-power cartidges, they are similar to many ways to submachine guns. However, compared to SMGs autocarbines typically have a lower rate of fire and are less compact, to allow for longer ranges, higher accuracy, and higher reliability. 
Autocarbines might not be the most powerful of weapons but their compact size and versatility make the useful for combat operations where space and weight are at a premium.
