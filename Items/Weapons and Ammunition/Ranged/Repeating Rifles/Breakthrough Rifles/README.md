Breakthrough rifles are oversized battle rifles designed for high-power cartridges. While immensely powerful, their size, weight, and especially recoil make them generally impractical for the majority of users. Weapons of this type are typically only used by cybernetically or genetically modified individuals with the strength to manage the gun's massive recoil. 
- Typical die size: D10
- Typical RoF: 3
- Typical capacity: 10
- Recoil: extreme
- Range: medium
