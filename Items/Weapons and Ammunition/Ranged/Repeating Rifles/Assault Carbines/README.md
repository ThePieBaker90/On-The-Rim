Assault carbines are small, compact repeating rifles. Firing low-power cartidges, they are similar to many ways to submachine guns. However, compared to SMGs assault carbines typically have a lower rate of fire and are less compact; to allow for longer ranges, higher accuracy, and higher reliability. Assault carbines might not be the most powerful of weapons but their compact size and versatility make the useful for combat operations where space and weight are at a premium. 
- Typical die size: D4
- Typical RoF: 3
- Typical capacity: 20
- Recoil: low
- Range: medium
