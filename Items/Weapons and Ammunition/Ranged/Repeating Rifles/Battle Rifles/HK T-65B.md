## General Description
The Howard-Kasseler Type 5365 rifle is a precision battle rifle chambered in 7.92 Empire. Offering consistent and reliable performance as well as slightly increased range over its competitors, the T-65 is an effective but somewhat expensive battle rifle.

## Lore
Developed by Robert Howard and Euridice Kasseler from 5359, the weapon was unveiled in 5363. Shooting trials showed a remarkable accuracy and reliability and orders for the rifle quickly flowed in. However, Howard and Kasseler were amateur gunmakers with no mass-production capabilities, and it was only in 5365 that the rifle and its designers would be picked up by the major weapons group Victory Weapons. The T-65 has since become one of Victory Weapons' best-selling products. The B-mod entered production in 5378 and features an additional underbarrel attachment point as well as a plasteel stock in place of the original synwood.
