## General Description
The Königs Arms K34 is an assault rifle chambered in the prorpietary 7.62 Königs Long. Similar to the preceding K88, the K34 has a sparse and rugged design. Being relible in all environments and easy to maintain, the K34 is most popular in the Outer Regions.

## Lore
The K34 was developed in 5432. It was designed for the relatively new 7.62x55mm "Königs Long" ammunition, a higher-power development of the 7.62x32mm "Königs Short", initially developed for the K04 rifle. The K34 was largely based on the K88 but features automatic instead of burst fire. This decision was based partly on an assesment that users choosing the higher power of the K34 over the K88 would also likely prefer a higher fire rate, and partly because space and weight limitations owuld make a burst mechanism expensive to produce. The V2 variant of 5397 allows for the fitting of a scope or other optics for longer-range operation.
