## General Description
The Armstek Model 5299 Battle Rifle is a very popular firearm. Chambered in 7.92 Empire, the M299 was developed as a heavier counterpart to the sucessful M223 Assault Rifle. Relatively affordable, reliable, powerful, and with just as much modularity as the M223, the M229 has seen great commerical sucess. However, it was never as successful as its older, smaller sibling; customers coming to Armstek for their affordable products are likely to purchase the lower-cost M223.

## Lore
Development began on the M299 in 5296. Its design was largely based on the M223, though it was not strictly a deerivative. Whereas the M223 was designed to Empire requirements, the M299 was a private venture. Production began in 5299 and in 5300 Brear Arms pitched the rifle to the Imperial Army as a replacement for the AR-8 battle rifle. The Army decided in 5303 to accept the rifle into service as the new standard battle rifle for heavy troops. Armstek, the producer of the AR series of rifles, protested, claiming that the acquisition of the M299 would give Brear Arms an unfair share of Imperial Army weapons purchases (since the M119 pistol and M223 assault rifle had already been standardized) and also violate Armstek's 5180 deal with the Army guaranteeing Armstek as the source of "flagship rifles" for the next quarter-millenium. After a lengthy legal battle, an agreement was made wherein Armtek would acquire the rights as sole manufacturer and distributer of both the M299 and the M223 rifles, also being responsible for any further development and marketting of the weapons. Brear Arms would meanwhile receive a 40% share in Armstek and exclusive rights as the Army's sole source of 5.45 Empire and 7.92 Empire ammunition to be used in the weapons.
