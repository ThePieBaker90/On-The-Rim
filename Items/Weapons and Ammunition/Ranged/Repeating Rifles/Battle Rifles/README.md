The battle rifle is the larger and heavier counterpart to the assault rifle. Firing full-powered cartridges, they provide significantly increased power, damage, and range than assault rifles. However, this comes at the cost of increased recoil and decreased capacity. Nonetheless they are immensely popular for military and paramilitary groups.
- Typical die size: D8
- Typical RoF: 3
- Typical capacity: 15
- Recoil: high
- Range: medium
