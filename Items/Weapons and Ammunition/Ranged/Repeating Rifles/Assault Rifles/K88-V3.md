## General Description
The Königs Arms K88 is an assault rifle produced by Königs Arms for their proprietary 7.62x32mm cartridge. A very sparse and rugged design, its higher calibre allows for increased power compared to most other assault rifles though at the expense of capacity.

## Lore
The Königs-88 was developed in 5388. An extremely simple stamped-metal design, it was designed for cheap production and easy maintenence. Königs guns are typically used in the Outer Regions, so the K88 was intended to be repaired with only basic tools while operating in any environment, even after significant exposure to the elements. The V3 variant allows fitting for a scope or other optics, though the mediocre long-range performance of the rifle makes such an addition somewhat redundant.
