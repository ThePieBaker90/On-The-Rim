## General Description
The Armstek Model 5223 assault rifle is an extremely popular firearm. Well-regarded for its affordability, reliability, all-around performance, and compatability with a wide array of modifications and add-ons. Hundreds of millions have been sold over its centuries of production, making the M223 possibly the best-selling firearm of all time. And yet they still don't offer a damn left-handed model.

## Lore
The Model 5223 was developed starting that same year by Brear Arms. It was marketted for the Project 5225 arms competition, where the Imperial Army was looking for a replacement for the AR-7E, AR-6G-2, and M/44 rifles then in service with a universal rifle chambered in the new 5.45 Empire cartridge. The Model 5223 was designed as efficiently and conventionally as possible, creating a lightweight, reliable, and affordable rifle with extensive upgrade and modification potential. Contracts for M223 production began in late standard August 5225, and the rifle was subsequently offered up for commercial sale, where it really found its greatest success. Over the last two centuries the basic design has hardly changed, a testament to its efficiency. From 5305, the rifle has been produced by Armstek.
