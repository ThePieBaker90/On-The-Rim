## General Description
The Armstek Model 5318 is a robust and reliable service rifle. Its design is centered around simplicity and reliability to minimize operator workload. With a comparable cost to the Model 5306 Battle Rifle and Model 5308 Marksman Rifle but lower performance than either, the M318 is not especially popular for professional military forces. However, its simplicity is valued for many secondary forces and corporate militias where heavy firepower is not necessary and operator training is less extensive.

## Lore
An internal Armstek study conducted in 5310 determined that many operators of Armstek products were poorly-trained and largely disposable company enforcers. These enforcers were insufficiently trained to operate and maintain their rifles, resulting in poor reviews of these products. A proposed solution was to make a "dummy-proof" rifle with as simple operation and maintenence as possible. Development was extremely protracted as Armstek struggled to design this new class of weapon. Finally in 5317 the Model 5318 was unveiled. Howard-Kasseler promplty sued for stolen technology, citing the M318's striking resemblance to their own Type 90 marksman rifle as well as a 5316 break-in at a HK archives facility. However, insufficient evidence was found to prove that Armstek actually stole HK technology, and the suit was disposed by Imperial mediators in 5324.
