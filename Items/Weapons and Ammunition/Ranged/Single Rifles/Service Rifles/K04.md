## General Description
The Königs Arms K04 is a service rifle chambered in the proprietary 7.62 Königs Long, a cartridge originally developed specifically for the K04. A robust long rifle, the K04 was originally intended for settlers as a personal weapon but has since been picked up by military and paramilitary groups as the concept of aa service rifle gained popularity with the introduction of the M418.

## Lore
In the late 54th century, a Königs study found that while their 7.92x32mm cartridge was considerably more popular than the competing 5.45 Empire, the 7.92 Empire cartridge was widely used in a variety of weapons, paarticularly simple single-shot rifles. Getting into the bolt-action market would be too difficult due to its heavy saturation, but Königs engineers believed there was an opening in the market for semi-automatic multipurpose rifles for defense, hunting, and combat. In 5401 a new 7.62x55mm cartridge was created by simply lengthening the 7.62x32mm cartridge, and shortly afterwards a simple, low-cost semi-auto rifle was developed to fire it. Entering production in 5404, the K04 was a smash hit seeing as its only major competitor was the Huntsman Semi Mk III. The success of the K04 resulted in the development of a number of additional weapons to take advantage of the new "Königs Long" cartridge.
