Marksman rifles are rifles desinged for high accuracy and long range while reatining a typical form factor. In many cases these weapons are modified battle rifles. Designed for sniping rather than assault, these weapons are typically semi-automatic. Damage output might be lower than other weapon types but marksman rifles are still a highly valued weapon type thanks to their ability to outrange most opponents. 

Typical die size: D8
Typical RoF: 1
Typical capacity: 15
Range: long
