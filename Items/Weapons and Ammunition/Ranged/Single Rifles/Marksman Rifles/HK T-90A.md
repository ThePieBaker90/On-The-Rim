## Lore
With the success of the Type 5365 rifle, Victory Weapons executives urged Howard and Kasseler to design a new rifle. With the accuracy of the T-65 being its biggest selling point, there was a significant impetus to produce a marksman rifle. Thus development began in 5386 on what would become the Type 90 marksman rifle. When unveiled in 5390, the T-90A was immediately successful and praised for its accuracy, reliability, and performance. The commercial success of the T-90 resulted in establishment of an independent Howard-Kasseler division of VW. This would provide the designers with much greater resources for further development of more rifles. It was around this time that Robert Howard's health began to decline.
