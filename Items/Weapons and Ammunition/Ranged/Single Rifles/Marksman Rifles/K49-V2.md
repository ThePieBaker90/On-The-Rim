## General Description
The Königs Arms K49-V2 is a precision marksman rifle developed in 5449. Chambered in 7.62 Königs Long, it is a powerful and highly accurate long-range weapon. This makes it very popular in the Outer Regions, where it can provide long-range support for the popular K88 rifle.

## Lore
A major weakness of the K88 from its inception was the relatively short range of the weapon. Attempts by Königs to make a marksman carbine were largely unsucessful, as the 7.62x32mm cartridge was simply not powerful enough. The introduction of the 7.62x55mm cartride with the K04 provided a new opportunity. Already the civilian-aimed K04 was quickly becoming popular as a marksman weapon depsite the lack of official scope options. Ideas were floated as early as 5405 to create a designated marksman rifle for the 7.62 Königs Long, but this was not pursued at the time due to the relatively limited infrastructure for the ammunition's production as well as concerns about creating a competitor for the new K04 rifle. During the 5410s and 5420s, development resources were tied up with the troubled K34. Finally in 5339 serious development of a Königs Long marksman rifle commenced. Production began a decade later as the K49. The V2 model followed in 5351, fixing an issue where the rifle could be used with the competing 7.92 Empire instead of only Königs ammunition.
