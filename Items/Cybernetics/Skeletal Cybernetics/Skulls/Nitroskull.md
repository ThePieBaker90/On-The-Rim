* Install Location:
	* [[Skull]]
- [[Cybernetics#Cyber Modifier|Cyber Modifier]]:
	* +2.
* [[Cybernetics#Affected Skills/Ability Scores|Affected Skills/Ability Scores]]:
	* +Intimidation.
	* -Persuasion.
* Cost:
	* 500 credits.
* [[Cybernetics#Cybercost|Cybercost]]:
	* 4.
* Abilities:
	* The user may drink 500mL of [[Chemfuel]] to get the following effects for 10 turns:
		* They double their [[Movement Speed|Movement Speed]].
		* All of their melee attacks deal an extra 1d6 force damage.
		* When they attack with a ranged weapon, the [[Rate of Fire]] of the weapon is increased by 1 for the attack.
		* Every turn they take 1d4 poison damage and 1d4 fire damage.
		* They have advantage on all dexterity checks.
* Looks:
	* A dark steel skull with several exhaust pipes spouting from the top. A transparent pipe with an orange liquid comes out of each side and proceeds to go back in. Siphons off ingested gasoline to power up the user, although the gasoline's toxicity and the burning skull are not protected against.
