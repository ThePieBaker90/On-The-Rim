A piece of armor that has the Warcasket property must be installed on the user rather than put on. Once installed, the Warcasket becomes part of the creature’s body and the creature loses their [[Arm|Arms]] and [[Leg|Legs]]. Arms and legs are not needed when a Warcasket is installed and do not incur a penalty for being missing.

When installed, the creature may use Warcasket weapons.
