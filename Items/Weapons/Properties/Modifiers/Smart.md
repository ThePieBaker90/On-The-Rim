* Cost:
	* +60% cost
* Usability:
	* Usable on all firearms
* Effects:
	* Weapons with the smart property require that its user have the ability to interface with smart weapons (such as with a [[Smart Implant]]).
	* When attacking a creature with the smart property, the defending creature's effective [[Evade Class]] is halved (with the result rounded up). 
* Looks:
	* Weapons with this modifier have a digital sight which shows the current target.
